About Me

I am Mohammad Mahdi Ershadi, an Industrial Engineering researcher affiliated with Amirkabir University of Technology, Tehran, Iran.

I received my Bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering from Isfahan University of Technology in 2015 and my Master's degree in Industrial Engineering and Management Systems from Amirkabir University of Technology in 2017.

Since 2017, I have been actively engaged in academic research at Amirkabir University of Technology. My research focuses on the intersection of data science, operations research, system analysis, and healthcare systems.

I have also contributed to the scientific community through peer-review activities for international academic journals.
